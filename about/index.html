About This Resource

A Plain-Language Bridge method for reading labels, built around a shared CNShopper spreadsheet.

What this resource is

This is an independent informational and navigation resource for people who use a CNShopper spreadsheet. It offers a Plain-Language Bridge — keep the original wording, write a cautious plain note, mark the unknown, and re-check — so a label you do not fully understand is read carefully instead of guessed.

What this resource is not

This resource is not the official CNShopper website, and it is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by CNShopper or Google. It is not a translator, a product database, a store, a QC certification, a shopping agent, an order tracker, or a fee calculator.

The Plain-Language Bridge

Everything here follows four moves: Keep Original (preserve the visible wording), Write Plain (a cautious note in clear English), Mark Unknown (flag ambiguity and missing context), and Recheck (return to the actual page and option). A note only helps you read; it does not prove an option, material, size, price, stock, seller, or platform status.

How to use it

  1. Open the shared Google Sheet to see the listing references.
  2. Browse the twelve product category dividers from the home page.
  3. Read the three guides before relying on any label.
  4. Keep the original, write plain, mark unknown, and re-check the page.

Reading example — not translation output, product verification, or a saved note.
